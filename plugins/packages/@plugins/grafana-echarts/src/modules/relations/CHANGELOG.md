## 0.0.1

- The panel adds graph, Sankey, and chord visualizations for connected data.
- Presets provide starting points for service maps, circular networks, weighted flows, mutual relations, and time networks.
- Grafana field configuration controls value mappings, tooltips, data links, legends, and colors.
- Panel configuration controls graph layouts, labels, time navigation, interactions, and data safety limits.
